Brand kit

The colours, type and logos used on flexfusionx.com.au. Use these for Instagram, flyers, slides and signage so everything looks like one brand: dark, cinematic, black and white, with one red accent.

01 · Logo

Logo

Lockup, whiteDefault. On black, dark photos and video.
Lockup, blackOn white and light backgrounds.
FX markSmall spaces: avatars, favicons, stickers, watermarks.
WordmarkWide formats: banners, headers, email signatures.
Mark on Signal RedOccasional accent only, such as a sticker or story highlight.
Red markUse sparingly, as a bullet or detail. Never as the main logo.

Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the FX mark's crossbar. Don't stretch, rotate, recolour (other than black, white or Signal Red), outline or add effects.

04 · Voice

Do and don't

Short and direct

"Rehab that works around your training." One idea per line. Talk like a coach, not a brochure.

Black and white first

Let the photo carry the mood. Red is a detail, not a fill.

Headlines in caps

Big, tight, uppercase Inter Black. Everything else in sentence case.

Outcome promises

AHPRA rules: no "guaranteed results", no testimonials about clinical outcomes, no before/after claims.

Extra colours

No gradients, blues or greens. Ink, white and Signal Red only.

Wall of text

If a post needs more than three short lines, split it into a carousel.
